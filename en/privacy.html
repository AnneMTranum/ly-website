Privacy policy

Last updated: 7 November 2026

Ly's privacy policy and terms are available in English and Danish. This English version is the governing version; if the two differ, the English version applies. This doesn't affect your rights as a consumer.

In short

Ly is built so your information stays with you. What you write in Ly is kept on your own phone. There's no account, no ads, no analytics and no tracking.

If you choose to share something with your close ones, exactly that – and only that – is sent encrypted through your own iCloud accounts with Apple. We can't see your information.

Who is the controller

The controller is IV Technologies Ltd, registered in England and Wales with company number 17316561. If you have questions about your information, write to support@ly-app.info.

What information Ly processes

Ly only processes what you write or choose in the app yourself:

  • Your name or nickname and a profile photo, if you choose one.
  • Your cycle: periods, bleeding and the predictions Ly works out.
  • Your check-ins: mood, energy, symptoms and how much they affect you, notes, habits and pills.
  • Plans in the calendar, "What helps me" (text and possibly a photo) and your reminders.
  • Your close ones: the name and relationship you give them, and what each of them may see.

Information about your cycle, your mood and your symptoms is health data. It's a special category of personal data, and that's why Ly asks for your explicit consent before you start.

Ly doesn't ask for your email, your phone number, any national ID number or your location.

Why, and on what legal basis

Ly uses your information to show you your own patterns, predict your cycle, remind you of what you've chosen, and share what you choose with your close ones.

The legal basis is your explicit consent, which you give on the "Your data is yours" screen before you use the app. You can withdraw it at any time under Settings → Your consent. Ly then deletes your check-ins, periods and what you share.

If you're one of the close ones, you give your consent on the "Before you start" screen before you connect. It covers your name, your photo and what you send. If you disconnect, Ly asks again next time.

Where your information is kept

  • On your phone: everything you write in Ly is saved in the app's own area on the phone. Ly doesn't sync your check-ins to iCloud or any server.
  • Your phone's backup: if you've turned on iCloud Backup or a backup on a computer in your iPhone's settings, Ly's data is included, just like for other apps. That's controlled by Apple and you.
  • A copy you save yourself: if you use "Save a copy of your data", you decide where the file goes, e.g. in Files.

What leaves your phone

Ly only sends something from the phone in these cases:

  • What you choose to share with your close ones, and what they send you – encrypted through your iCloud with Apple.
  • The invitation code – a small record with Apple that's deleted as soon as someone has connected.
  • Buying Ly Plus – the App Store checks whether you have a subscription. We aren't told who you are.
  • When you tap a link yourself, e.g. to Samaritans, Shout, Livslinien or "Send feedback", it opens outside Ly, in the phone, the browser or your email.

Everything else – your check-ins, periods, notes, habits and reminders – stays on the phone.

When you share with your close ones

When you invite a close one, Ly creates a shared area in your private iCloud with Apple (CloudKit). The area only contains what you've chosen that this person may see – e.g. the phase, mood and energy, symptoms, notes, plans, "reach out" messages, your profile photo and today's selfie. The content is encrypted so only the two of you can read it.

Your close one can send replies, notes, their profile photo and a selfie back. Messages and selfies disappear after a day, and replies and notes are removed from iCloud as soon as they've reached your phone.

The invitation is a link from Apple and a code. The code points to the link through a small record with Apple, which is deleted as soon as someone has connected. If you stop sharing with a person, the shared area is deleted, and the person can no longer see anything from you.

Without Ly Plus, only the phase is shared.

iCloud is Apple's service, which you and your close ones use under your own agreement with Apple. Apple may store the content on servers outside the UK and the EU under Apple's privacy policy, but it's encrypted, so neither Apple nor we can read it.

Permissions on the phone

Ly only asks for access when you use a feature that needs it. You can always turn access off again in your iPhone's Settings → Ly.

  • Notifications: for your reminders and for messages from your close ones.
  • Camera: only for today's selfie. The photo is taken in Ly and isn't saved to your photo library.
  • Photos: for your profile photo and the photo in "What helps me". You choose one photo yourself, and Ly doesn't see the rest of your library.
  • Face ID: to lock Ly. Ly is only told whether it worked – never anything about your face.

Apple and crash reports

  • Apple runs the App Store, iCloud and the messages that wake Ly when a close one has written. Apple processes information under its own privacy policy: apple.com/uk/legal/privacy.
  • If you've turned on "Share With App Developers" in your iPhone's settings, Apple sends us anonymous reports if Ly crashes. They don't contain what you've written in Ly, and you can turn it off in your iPhone's settings under Privacy & Security → Analytics & Improvements.

Ly contains no advertising, analytics or tracking tools from anyone else.

Purchases and subscription

Ly Plus is bought and paid for through Apple. Apple handles payment, renewal, cancellation and refunds, and we never see your payment details. If Ly Plus ends, nothing is deleted – your data stays on the phone.

How long

Your information stays on your phone for as long as you have Ly and don't delete it yourself. Messages and selfies to and from your close ones disappear after a day. If you delete the app, the information on the phone is deleted; a backup you've made is up to you.

Your rights

You have the right of access, rectification, erasure, data portability and to withdraw your consent. Because your information is only on your phone, we can neither see, hand over nor delete it for you – you do that yourself in Ly, where you can:

  • see everything you've written and correct it,
  • save a copy of your data (Settings → Your data),
  • stop sharing with a close one,
  • delete all your data (Settings → Your data → Delete all data) or withdraw your consent, which also deletes it.

If you delete the app, everything in Ly on the phone disappears. If you're one of the close ones, you can disconnect at any time in Settings. Your profile photo and your selfie are then also removed from the phone of the person you supported.

If you've written to us, you can always ask to see or have your emails deleted. If you have questions, write to support@ly-app.info. You can also complain to the UK Information Commissioner's Office (ico.org.uk) or, in Denmark, to Datatilsynet (datatilsynet.dk).

Age limit

Ly is for you if you're 16 or older. You confirm your age when you give your consent.

Security

Ly's data is kept in the app's protected area on the phone. You can lock Ly with Face ID. What you share is encrypted when it's sent and stored in iCloud.

The website ly-app.info

The website uses no cookies, no analytics and no ads. The fonts are hosted on our own site, so your browser doesn't fetch anything from others. The site is hosted by GitHub Pages, which may briefly store technical information such as your IP address for security reasons. GitHub is American, is certified under the EU-US Data Privacy Framework and processes the information under its own privacy policy.

Changes

If we change the policy significantly, we'll tell you in the app before it takes effect.
