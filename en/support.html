FAQ

Here are answers to the most common questions.
You'll find more in the app under Settings → Help and support → Help & FAQ.

What can Ly help me with?

Ly helps you understand how your cycle affects your mood, your energy and your body. Over time, Ly learns which symptoms you most often notice in the different phases, so you can plan around it – and share it with the people close to you.

Where is my data?

Only on your phone. Ly has no account and doesn't send your data anywhere unless you share a report yourself or choose to share with your close ones.

How do I share with my close ones?

Under Close ones in the app you can invite up to 3 people. You choose what each of them sees, and you can always stop sharing.

What is Ly Plus?

Ly is free to use. Ly Plus gives you Insights, all your earlier cycles, "usual" notes, plans in Calendar, reports and up to 3 close ones who can see more. £22.99 a year or £4.99 a month, with a 7-day free trial.

How do I cancel Ly Plus?

In your Apple Account: Settings on your iPhone → your name → Subscriptions → Ly Plus. Your data isn't deleted if you stop.

I've got a new phone – how do I bring in my data?

Save a copy in Ly on the old phone (Settings → Your data → Save a copy of your data), and restore it on the new one under Your data → Restore a copy. If you use iCloud Backup, your data comes along too.

Is Ly a medical device?

No. Ly is a wellbeing companion and doesn't make diagnoses. Your check-ins can be a good starting point for a talk with your doctor.
