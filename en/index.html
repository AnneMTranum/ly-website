Simple when things are hard

On the difficult days, an app shouldn't be one more task. One tap for mood, one for energy and a few for what you notice. That's all it takes.

Gets to know you

Over time, Ly learns which symptoms – both physical and mental – you most often notice in the different phases. So you can understand them and plan your everyday life around them.

Builds a bridge to your close ones

Ly gives you a shared language. Your close ones learn what you're going through and when you might need them.

When it storms

On the difficult days, there's help at hand. Exercises to find connection, your own toolbox of phrases or words that help you, and a way to reach out.

Your close ones

You're not alone in the storm

Ly is also made for communicating. Your close ones learn much more about what you go through in your cycle, and when you may find things especially hard and need a little extra support. Think of it as an anchor for you both in the storm.

  • They see what phase you're in and get small tips on what might help
  • They get a gentle reminder ahead of your difficult days
  • You reach out with a tap – and they can reply with a tap

You decide what each of them sees, and you can always stop sharing again. It's free for your close ones.

Privacy

Your data is yours

What you tell Ly is health information. That's why Ly is built so it stays with you.

Read the privacy policy
  • Everything stays on your phone. There's no account, and we can't see your data.
  • You decide what your close ones see – and you can always stop sharing again.
  • No ads and no tracking. Ly is funded by Ly Plus – not by ads.
  • You can always save a copy of your data or delete everything.
